📐 Mathematics

JSS 1 — First Term Scheme of Work

Week 1

Topic: Number and Numeration

Objectives: Students should understand basic number concepts and identify different types of numbers.

Week 2

Topic: Whole Numbers

Objectives: Students should be able to read, write and compare whole numbers.

Week 3

Topic: Factors and Multiples

Objectives: Students should identify factors and multiples of numbers.

Week 4

Topic: Fractions

Objectives: Students should identify and perform basic operations with fractions.

Week 5

Topic: Decimals

Objectives: Students should understand decimal notation and basic operations.
